The Upper Atmosphere Research Satellite (UARS) was a NASA Earth observatory that studied the Earth's atmosphere and changes in the ozone layer. It was launched from Space Shuttle Discovery on September 1991, and operated for 14 years until it was decommissioned in December 2005. It reentered the atmosphere in 2011.
