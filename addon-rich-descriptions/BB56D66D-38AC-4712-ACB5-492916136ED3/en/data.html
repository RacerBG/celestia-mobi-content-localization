INFINITY, a recreation of the trajectory designed and used in the YouTube video ""All planets flyby with only 3805ΔV in KSP(RSS+Principia)". Yes. This single spacecraft flies by every single planet in the Solar System. Launched in 1970 towards Mars and with 5 or 6 Venus assists it is able to visit every planet in the system culminating in a fly-by of Triton in 1984.
