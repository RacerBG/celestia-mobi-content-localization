Martian Moons eXploration (MMX) is a JAXA robotic mission to return samples from Phobos, the largest of two moons of Mars. Launched in October 2026, it is expected to arrive by August 2027 and will survey the moon in a series of quasi-satellite orbits close to Phobos, before attempting to land and gather Phobos samples by 2029. It will return at least 10 g of Phobos material back to Earth by 2031.

Note

Landing and return phases of the mission are not yet simulated, nor its separation sequences of major components. It will be added in a future update/release.
